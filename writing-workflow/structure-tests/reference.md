# Introduction

This synthetic study compares two language models for feedback generation. It asks how closely their feedback resembles teacher reference feedback under two input conditions. It does not measure learning outcomes.

# Methods

## Data

The same four student answers and teacher references are used for both models and both conditions.

## Evaluation

The study scores similarity with a text-comparison metric. Larger scores mean greater similarity, not necessarily greater educational usefulness.

### Metric definition

For this demonstration, the score is the number of matching words divided by the number of candidate words.

### Worked example

The reference is "answer needs evidence" and the candidate is "answer needs examples". Two of the three candidate words match, giving a score of 2/3.

## Comparison

The mean score is reported for each model and condition. Individual scores remain available to show where the means differ.

# Results and conclusion

The observed scores answer the comparison question. Any conclusion is limited to similarity on the four tested answers.
