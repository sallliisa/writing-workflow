# Structural assessment of the frozen thesis

The main comparison is traceable from the question in §1.2 to the four rates in §5.1. The six task groups explain where the ten failures occurred. Chapter III also gives two worked examples with different jobs. T1-C2 shows how six observations of 10 seconds and one of 2 seconds yield a reference mean of 8.86 seconds. T5-C1 shows how a returned object receives a verdict against a fixed reference. Both examples belong in the methods.

Two local repairs materially improve that chain. In §3.1 the wording can give the impression that the models use the expected answers. §§1.5 and 3.2.3 establish that the evaluator uses them and the agents cannot read them. In Table 3.4 the monthly coverage of vehicle classes and the eligibility filter for accident causes need to be visible before the reader encounters the failures in Chapter IV. Those rules are already documented in §§4.4–4.5.

The repetition results answer two questions. The number of passes per run describes performance across the fifteen cases. The three verdicts for one case show whether its outcome changed. Without context, both models passed every run on twelve cases, but only one model failed the same case all three times. A reader can derive this from §§4.4, 4.5 and 4.7. Summarizing it in §4.9 makes the conclusion easier to check. The study's exact-match criterion measures a returned answer. A description of how an agent reached that answer requires separate evidence, whose provenance the supplied methods leave open.

The reference's sequence from concepts to scoring examples and then group comparisons serves the target. Its persona experiments, course divisions, and text-similarity calculations have no direct counterpart in the target design.
