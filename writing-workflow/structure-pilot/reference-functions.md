# Reference functions — bounded workflow pilot

The reference evaluates generated academic feedback against reference feedback. Its research question names prompting, persona, and model comparisons (reference.md:25–33). Its own limits distinguish text similarity from pedagogical value (47–53). The target instead asks whether structured analytical answers agree with expected results. The useful correspondence is to explain what counts as success, make the evaluation inspectable, and constrain what the resulting numbers establish.

## Useful organization and limits

Keep the separation between conceptual justification and study procedure. The target already has this separation. Its extra subsection on repeat consistency serves the three-run experiment even though the selected reference subtrees have no equivalent. The target's exact-match example needs less mathematical machinery than four text metrics. Its 62/7 expected-result calculation is a distinct prerequisite, not a missing fifth metric subsection.

Do not transfer the reference's unsupported explanatory shortcuts. In §3.5.3 the four chunks are assumed (reference.md:697) and the later interpretation attributes the higher score to semantic matching without showing synonym or stemming matches in that demonstration (675–709). In §3.5.4 the scores are called estimates from a library (729–747), but this subsection does not supply a reproducible configuration for those numbers. Such passages model the intended explanatory sequence, not proof that more detail necessarily establishes a calculation. The METEOR prose at 320 also describes multiplying by the penalty while the displayed formula at 312 uses one minus the penalty. These are source-local issues detectable in the supplied text and are not a comprehensive technical validation of the reference metrics.

The scoped ATX hierarchy matches the visible sections. Tables, equation blocks, examples, and the numbered BERTScore components are assessed within their containing nodes. Those components are parts of one computation, not additional independent sections. No change to the frozen inventory is needed for this pilot.

The independent challenge confirmed the functional mappings and narrowed R-0041 as described above. Its additional source-local token-count observation is documented in [challenge.md](challenge.md); it creates no target requirement.
