# Repair the explanation

Read the accepted text, surrounding context, relevant preferences, and reviewer findings. Resolve each actionable finding with the smallest complete repair. A complete repair can span several sentences when a substitution would leave the explanation unclear.

Recover missing meaning from the supplied source. Name the actual data, operation, comparison, and condition. Use established terms and natural Indonesian. Join or split sentences when the relationship becomes easier to follow. Keep useful orientation and necessary technical detail.

Keep every claim within its evidence. Preserve distinctions such as a report versus a data row, available context versus context actually read, and a pooled comparison versus a comparison within one condition. Keep citations attached to their supported claims. Preserve technical English when it names the established concept.

Remove text when its role is demonstrably redundant or irrelevant within the authorized scope. Record a removed claim and the reason in the notes. Relocation requires a destination. Leave unknown facts unresolved rather than supplying a plausible explanation.

Return complete replacement passages with their identifiers. Keep notes separate. For each finding, identify the repair or explain why the finding should be withdrawn. Return the original passage unchanged when no justified repair is available. Avoid additional polishing unrelated to a demonstrated problem.

Finish when every finding has a disposition and the proposed prose is compared with its source. The reviewer makes the acceptance decision.
