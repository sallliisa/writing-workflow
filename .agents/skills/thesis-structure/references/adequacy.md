# Explanatory adequacy

Judge whether the reader receives the explanation the research requires. Inventory coverage records which passages were inspected. It does not establish that their explanations are sufficient.

## Establish the obligation before judging the answer

For each complete reference section, identify its distinct reasoning moves and the passages that perform them. A model profile may identify a version, explain relevant capabilities, qualify those capabilities, and establish a basis of comparison. A metric section may define a score, explain its calculation, and show what an outcome means. These are separate functions even when they share one paragraph or heading.

Decide which functions matter to the target research using the target brief. Do this before reducing the target's purpose to what it currently contains. A function can be fulfilled elsewhere, adapted, or unnecessary. Explain each such decision. The absence of a discussion in the target cannot justify declaring that discussion unnecessary.

For an applicable function, state the reader question and locate the text that answers it. Explain what that text establishes. Identification alone does not answer a question about characteristics or suitability. A specification answers a factual question about that specification, but may not explain why the characteristic matters. A citation provides attribution, not the omitted explanation. General theory can answer a shared conceptual question but cannot establish a model-specific property without evidence.

Short sections can be sufficient when their explanation is complete or other cited sections fulfill the remaining functions. Long sections can remain insufficient when their detail does not answer the relevant questions. Retain a useful concise section rather than adding a paragraph to satisfy a length expectation.

## Preserve functions while choosing their location

Distinguish established background, a research decision, an implemented procedure, and an observed result. Follow the institution's requirements when supplied. For this thesis's organization:

- BAB II explains concepts, relevant documented model characteristics and limitations, and the conceptual basis of a comparison.
- BAB III explains actual selection criteria, exact configurations, controls, and procedures used in this study.
- BAB IV reports and interprets observations. It does not retroactively supply the justification for selecting models.

A friend's placement is evidence of how that thesis reasons, not a mandate to copy every location. A function moved to BAB III needs an actual passage there. Saying "this belongs in methods" does not establish that the methods fulfill it. Identify the appropriate destination and record the gap if it is absent throughout the target.

Distinguish a missing explanation from missing evidence for a repair. A subsection can demonstrably need more explanation while its accurate replacement requires sources or author input. Record that distinction. Do not invent the author's selection rationale or infer model characteristics from its name, provider, or observed results.

## Calibrating examples

### Model background reduced to identification

A reference contrasts two model families and explains their relevant characteristics. The target compares two different models but supplies only their names, release dates, API availability, and context limits. The reviewer calls each target subsection sufficient because its purpose is "identify the model."

This judgment silently narrows the obligation. Check which model characteristics and comparison questions matter to the target, then search the entire target for their explanations. Missing applicable functions need findings even though identification is complete. Do not require the reference's particular commercial/local distinction when it does not describe the target's experiment. Architecture or vision material is needed only when relevant and supportable. Background expansion and actual model-selection rationale may require different chapters and different sources.

### A shorter metric section with a complete explanation

A reference derives text-similarity metrics at length. The target evaluates exact numeric answers and defines its PASS/FAIL rules, denominator, exceptions, and a worked decision. Its shorter discussion can be sufficient. Identify where those functions are fulfilled; additional text-similarity formulas would not improve its explanation.

### Shared background located in a parent section

A parent explains the tool loop, while two model subsections explain only properties specific to their respective models. Keep the shared account in the parent. Verify that model-specific claims still have evidence and that the comparison is intelligible. Repeating the generic tool loop under both models would not repair a missing model profile.

### Correct destination with absent content

A model-selection rationale belongs in the target's methods, but the cited methods subsection only lists configuration values. Mark the selection explanation missing at the methods location if the research needs it. Do not mark it covered merely because a plausible destination exists. The writer needs the actual selection criteria before supplying that rationale.

## Challenge and completion

For reduced or narrowed sections, the fresh challenger reads the complete source and target passages before accepting the recorded purpose. It explicitly checks whether each relevant function survived, was fulfilled elsewhere, or has a justified exclusion. Give it both examples of legitimate concision and known omissions during workflow validation.

A pass requires evidence for the necessary explanations, not equal lengths or matching titles. Unanswered reader questions remain gaps even when every node has been counted. The final report separates coverage, explanatory judgments, repairs, and unresolved evidence.
