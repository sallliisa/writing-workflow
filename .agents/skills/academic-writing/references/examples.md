# Editing decision examples

These are proposed illustrations, not user-endorsed ideal sentences. Use the current passage's evidence and the project's preference profile. Keep already-clear prose.

## Replace abstraction with a concrete operation

Source:

> Komponen ini menjelaskan unit satu baris data dan hubungan yang digunakan dalam pengelompokan.

Possible revision:

> Komponen ini menjelaskan apa yang diwakili oleh satu baris data dan hubungan antarentitas yang digunakan untuk mengelompokkan data.

The revision names the represented object. It still needs context to establish which component and relationships are intended. A fluent sentence with unresolved referents remains incomplete.

## Explain a necessary calculation

Source:

> Rata-rata dihitung dengan pembobotan observasi yang tidak seimbang.

Possible revision, only when the source establishes this calculation:

> Setiap laporan memuat jumlah data durasi yang berbeda. Rata-rata keseluruhan dihitung dengan membagi total durasi seluruh laporan dengan jumlah data durasi yang digunakan.

Name the data and operation rather than substituting another abstract label. Preserve any supported rule about null values. Do not infer that rule from this example.

## Preserve limits of evidence

Synthetic source:

> Model A memperoleh 18/20 PASS tanpa konteks dan 20/20 PASS dengan konteks. Hasil ini membuktikan bahwa konteks meningkatkan kemampuan model secara signifikan.

Possible revision when only descriptive counts are available:

> Model A memperoleh 18/20 PASS tanpa konteks dan 20/20 PASS dengan konteks. Selisih tingkat keberhasilannya adalah 10 poin persentase.

Record the unsupported causal and statistical claims separately. Preserve a supported interpretation when additional evidence establishes it.

## Choose what belongs

Synthetic source in a subsection about research data:

> Data penelitian disimpan dalam tabel. Tabel terdiri atas baris dan kolom.

The second sentence may add nothing for the intended reader. Remove it when it serves no explanatory purpose. Add detail about the actual data structure only when the analysis needs it and the source supports it.

## Preserve a complete explanation

When a passage already names the objects, explains the relevant relationship, and supplies necessary evidence, keep it. A proposed synonym, shorter sentence, or different rhythm needs a demonstrated reader benefit. A review can correctly produce no revision.
